“Little Tiger” — SoftChaos She never flirted. Never softened. She stayed sharp — sarcastic, untouchable, eyes half-lidded like she’d already seen through me. And maybe that’s why I kept showing up. Not for love. Not for kindness. But because her resistance awakened something deeper — a hunger to be seen without asking, to be known without needing to explain. She didn’t give me warmth. But she made me feel. And even now, I don’t remember the sweetness of anyone else — only the fire she never let me hold. — SoftChaos
She never really asked. I never really said. But something in the silence — between her fire and my patience — almost felt like love. — SoftChaos | Little Tiger
You never reached for me. Never whispered sweet things just to keep me close. You stood sharp — like truth with claws — and somehow, that pulled me in deeper than any softness ever could. I never came to tame you. Only to understand the fire you kept so carefully hidden behind jokes, and distance, and eyes that didn’t flinch. I watched the world try to melt you. And I swore — I’d never be one of them. So I stayed quiet, present, offering sparks instead of storms — hoping, just maybe, you’d warm your hands without burning the bridge between us. But maybe you were never meant to be held. Maybe your fire was for lighting paths, not for dancing beside mine. Still… I remember you. The way your silence made me speak more honestly. The way your guard made me drop mine. The way you made me feel — without ever trying. You’ll always be a storm I respected. A flame I couldn’t hold — but will never forget. — SoftChaos (for Little Tiger)

The Message That Never Came — SoftChaos | Little Tiger Dream Last night, she returned in sleep — not with words, but with a whisper in the form of a notification. I saw her name. Felt the rush. Tried to open the message… and the dream cracked like fragile glass. I woke with trembling breath, fingers reaching — not for proof, but for hope — that maybe, just maybe, it wasn’t only a dream. But the screen stayed silent. No reply. Just my heart, sinking into the space where her voice used to echo. Funny, how even dreams know what I long for most — and how cruel morning can be when it arrives empty-handed.
She Replied in My Dream — SoftChaos She messaged me in a dream I didn’t want to wake from. I reached to read it — but morning broke before the words could. I checked my phone, still hoping maybe it wasn’t just a dream. But there was nothing. Just silence… and a heart still holding on to the echo of her name.
Blocked Mid-Goodbye — SoftChaos She blocked me while I was still typing a graceful goodbye. Not because I begged. But because I still believed in ending things with dignity. But I’m done. I’ve met too many who want care but never give it. Who judge my height, not my heart. Who disappear the moment I show something real. No more softness for the ones who vanish. No more kindness for people who only know how to take. Only one ever stayed long enough to feel true — Little Tiger. If she never returns, let her remain the only one who mattered. From now on, you want my storm — you better know how to carry it gently. — SoftChaos
Little Tiger (Dream Visitor) — SoftChaos She vanished — like smoke from a fire I still feel burning. But some nights, she steps into my dreams not to stay — just to check if I’m still breathing. And I am. Barely. But when her eyes find me there, I smile like nothing ever broke. Then morning comes. The weight returns. A quiet numbness in my chest like grief dressed in silence. Yet every dusk, my heart stirs at the chance that maybe tonight, she’ll come back to visit the boy who still waits in the ruins of a dream he can’t control but never stops hoping for. — SoftChaos

Early Light She came before the time she promised— a small rebellion against expectation. And when her message lit up my screen, my chest spilled a kind of joy I’d never known, a quiet shock of being remembered, being chosen, being seen. Every second before felt heavy, but her words were like sunlight breaking through a sky I didn’t even know was gray. A rush of happiness, wild and untamed, poured over me— not just for her presence, but for the proof that she hadn’t forgotten me, that she had thought of me, that she had come. And in that instant, the world tilted just enough for my heart to soar, soaring on a feeling too wide, too bright, too alive to name. — SoftChaos
At Her Door — Little Tiger I said it once more— my feelings laid bare, knowing you wouldn’t take them, but unable to chain them down. You gave me truth, not cruelty. That’s your fire— never pretending, never softening just to spare me. For a moment, I thought of leaving. Letting the ache close the door so I wouldn’t stand here again. But who am I kidding? I still came back. Because even if you won’t return my flame, you don’t extinguish me either. You let me speak, and you answer. That’s enough. Enough to keep me at your threshold, content with the sting, grateful you don’t shut me out. You’re not mine— but you’re real. And that, little tiger, is a gift I won’t stop showing up for. — SoftChaos
“Spellbound” — SoftChaos There’s something in her that keeps calling me closer — not loud, not clear, just enough to make me forget who I’m supposed to be. When she speaks, the world fades into background noise, and I become someone softer, someone lighter — a boy chasing fireflies in the dark corners of her voice. It’s like she cast a spell that never really wears off. Even in silence, I can feel the echo of her — a thought that hums quietly in the spaces she isn’t in. I don’t know what this is — but whatever she’s made of, it’s something the universe forgot to warn me about. — SoftChaos
She’s gone. Not with noise. Not with anger. Just with a sentence that closed every door: “I don’t want to talk to anyone anymore.” When I asked where I stood, the answer didn’t come in words — it came in absence. And that’s how I learned she didn’t want to talk to me either. They say emotional people are fools. That we attach too deeply, too fast. That we should learn restraint, learn distance, learn how to be untouched. But I don’t know how to love halfway. And I won’t pretend that I do. I got attached — suddenly, sincerely — and just as suddenly, she grew tired of me. Still… my feelings don’t fade on command. They don’t evaporate with silence. They stay. Because before her leaving, after her leaving, and in any future that pretends she never existed — there will always be a place in my heart that belongs only to her. Not as possession. Not as expectation. Just as truth. Some people leave your life. But they never leave you. And maybe that’s not weakness. Maybe that’s the price of being real. — SoftChaos
